Our Story

Yakima's Home for
Real Espresso

The Mocha Tree Espresso has been serving Yakima for over twenty seven years — making us the valley's longest running espresso bar. What started as a single drive-thru window has grown into a community staple, one handcrafted drink at a time.

We remember how you take your coffee. We know your name. That's not a policy — it's just who we are.
